# Characters

The roster is home base: every character you track, grouped how you like, with
deep detail a click away.

## The roster

The roster shows a card per character. Group characters into squads and keep the
rest in the unassigned pool.

![Populated roster grid](/docs/img/characters/roster-grid.png)

![Squads and the unassigned pool](/docs/img/characters/squads.png)

## The character card

Each card surfaces the portrait, corporation, location, and skill-queue status.
