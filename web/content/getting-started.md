# Getting Started

Welcome to **Pod**, the free, native EVE Online companion. This page walks you
through installing Pod and adding your first character.

## Install Pod

Download the build for your platform and follow the installer prompts.

![Open Anyway on macOS](/docs/img/getting-started/install-macos.png)

![Windows installer](/docs/img/getting-started/install-windows.png)

![Linux AppImage launch](/docs/img/getting-started/install-linux.png)

## First launch

Pod shows a splash screen while it starts up, then drops you on an empty roster
ready for your first character.

![Pod splash screen](/docs/img/getting-started/splash.png)

![Empty first-run roster](/docs/img/getting-started/onboarding-empty.png)

## Add your first character

Pod reads your character data through the public ESI with your explicit consent.
Adding a character takes a single round-trip through EVE's SSO.

![Add a character panel](/docs/img/getting-started/auth-panel.png)

![Waiting for authorization](/docs/img/getting-started/auth-waiting.png)

![Roster with your first character](/docs/img/getting-started/roster-first-character.png)
