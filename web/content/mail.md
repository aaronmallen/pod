# Mail

Read, organize, and compose EVE mail without leaving Pod.

## Overview

Mail is a three-pane view: folders, the message list, and the open message.

![Mail three-pane view](/docs/img/mail/three-pane.png)

![Folder sidebar](/docs/img/mail/folders.png)

## Reading mail

Open a message to read its body, with inline overlays for linked calendar and
skill content.

![Open message detail](/docs/img/mail/message-detail.png)

![Empty folder state](/docs/img/mail/folder-empty.png)

## Composing and labeling

Compose new mail with entity search in the recipient field, and organize
messages with labels.

![Compose panel](/docs/img/mail/compose.png)

![Label picker](/docs/img/mail/labels.png)
