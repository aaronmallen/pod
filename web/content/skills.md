# Skills

Plan, track, and compare skill training across all your characters.

## Overview

The Skills view is a three-pane layout: the training queue, attributes, and a
right pane that switches between browse, attributes, and plans.

![Skills three-pane overview](/docs/img/skills/overview.png)

![Empty skill queue](/docs/img/skills/queue-empty.png)

## Right pane

Browse the skill catalog, review attributes, and manage saved plans.

![Browse the skill catalog](/docs/img/skills/browse.png)

![Attributes](/docs/img/skills/attributes.png)

![Saved plans list](/docs/img/skills/plans.png)

## Building plans

Multi-select queue rows to create a plan from your current selection.

![Multi-selected queue rows](/docs/img/skills/queue-multiselect.png)

![Skill plan editor](/docs/img/skills/plan-editor.png)

![Plan import and export](/docs/img/skills/plan-editor-importexport.png)

## Comparing characters

Compare skills across two or more characters in a single matrix.

![Skills compare window](/docs/img/skills/compare.png)
