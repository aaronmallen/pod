# Pod Docs

Pod is a companion app for EVE Online that runs on your own computer. You add
the characters you fly, and Pod pulls in their data from EVE so you can read it
without logging into the game. It is a desktop app for Windows, macOS, and
Linux, not a website and not an overlay.

These pages explain what Pod does and how each part of it works. If you have
just installed Pod, start with [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started/) to add
your first character.

## What Pod does

Pod gives you one place to check on your characters between play sessions. Once
a character is added, you can see:

- Your roster of characters, grouped into squads or left in a shared pool.
- Skills, including what each character has trained and how the queue is
  progressing.
- Mail, with the same inbox, folders, and labels you have in the game, plus the
  ability to read and send messages.
- Wallet activity, including your balance, the transaction journal, market
  orders, and contracts.
- Assets, so you can find what you own and where it is parked across stations
  and structures.
- A calendar of in-game events and Pod reminders, such as when a skill finishes
  or an industry job is ready.
- Industry, covering your jobs, blueprints, and a planner for working out what a
  build needs.
- A Captain's Log for writing up what each day brought, with room for a note on
  each of that day's calendar events.

You do not have to use all of it. Turn features on or off from Settings and keep
the parts you care about.

## Where your data lives

Pod talks to EVE through ESI, the official EVE Swagger Interface. When you add a
character, you sign in with your EVE account and grant Pod read access to that
character. From then on Pod syncs in the background and keeps a local copy of
the data on your machine.

That local copy is the important part. Pod reads from its own database, so the
app stays fast and works even when you are offline. EVE does not push changes to
you in real time, so what you see reflects the last sync. Pod refreshes on its
own schedule, and you can trigger a sync yourself when you want the latest.

Because everything lives on your computer, your character data is not stored on
a server you do not control. You decide which characters to add, and you can
remove a character to clear its data.

## Where to start

Add your first character with [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started/). After
that, the sidebar lists a page for each feature. Read the one that matches what
you want to do, or browse them in order to get the full picture.
